\begin{abstract}
Urban Beyond Visual Line of Sight (BVLOS) operations require Ground-Based Surveillance Systems (GBSS) that separately account for \emph{cooperative} traffic (e.g., Remote~ID / RF) and \emph{non-cooperative} dark targets (Radar, EO, Acoustic). Collapsing both into a single coverage score systematically understates dark-target risk. This paper revisits city-scale sensor placement with the SCOPAS dual-layer framework: multi-objective NSGA-II optimizing threat-weighted \(M_{wp,coop}\), \(M_{wp,noncoop}\), and CapEx under deterministic 3D LoS occlusion on OSM geometry, with Acoustic as a low-cost non-cooperative modality and explicit planning floors (e.g., \(0.90/0.35\)).

On a 1\,km$\times$1\,km synthetic city, cooperative coverage above \(0.96\) is reached in every run, whereas the best non-cooperative coverage ranges from \(0.41\) to \(0.51\) across three seeds (\(0.56\) on the front we show). The joint floor \(0.85/0.35\) is met in two of three seeds, at \$383k and \$600k. On a 30\,m Avenida Paulista corridor with 6,389 OSM buildings, five RF receivers reach \(0.93\) cooperative coverage for \$150k, but the best non-cooperative coverage is \(0.22\). Re-evaluating stored plans per airway shows that the same hardware covers \(39\)--\(52\%\) of the threat-weighted 20\,m layer cooperatively, against \(92\)--\(99\%\) at 45 and 65\,m. Three controls use the \emph{same} ray-tracer: (A)~on enumerable crops (\(n\leq12\)), a fixed-$k$ GA matches the oracle \(M_c\) within \(0.3\)\,pp; (B)~at matched CapEx, RF-only wins lean classic \(M_c\); (C)~in the dual-layer ablation, RF saturates \(M_{wp,coop}\) with zero non-cooperative coverage, and heterogeneous mixes win \(M_{wp,noncoop}\) only at \$320k: \(47.5\%\) with hardware-only budgets, \(39.0\%\) when each site costs \$15k. Heterogeneity is needed for joint dark-target floors, not for lean cooperative coverage. We do \emph{not} claim optimality on city-scale fronts. Code: \url{https://github.com/andrekuros/SCOPAS_Sensor_Placement}.
\end{abstract}
